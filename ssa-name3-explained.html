Populations: matching that knows the culture

Each population is a rule set for one country, language or region: its nicknames, spelling habits, name order and scripts. Informatica can also build custom populations. Examples below; the product installer shows the current list.

Across scripts

  • Roman ↔ local script, as in Greece or Israel
  • Chinese, Japanese and Korean, including Katakana with Roman
  • Bidirectional Arabic and Latin

Mixed data

  • One system can serve many countries
  • Searches in one language against a database built on international rules
  • Custom populations for unusual requirements

Where it fits with AI decision models

Fast decision models, such as OpenAI's new Decisions API, are strong at judging one pair. SSA-NAME3 is strong at everything around that judgment.

SSA-NAME3Decision model
Find candidates in millions of recordsYes — keys and search rangesNo — needs candidates supplied
Names across 60+ cultures and scriptsBuilt in, tested for decadesGeneral knowledge, untested per population
Same answer every timeDeterministicProbabilistic
Hard gray-zone judgmentsRoutes to a stewardStrong — a calibrated second opinion
Cost per million pairsLowHigh

Best together: SSA-NAME3 finds and scores candidates; a decision model adjudicates only the gray zone; MDM keeps the golden record and the audit trail.
